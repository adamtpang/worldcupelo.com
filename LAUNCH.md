# worldcupelo.com Launch Plan

Generated by running SellSniper's logic on worldcupelo.com (34 candidate rooms, adversarially verified, 8 real and self-promo-safe survived). Timed to the 2026 World Cup, which is live now. These posts are seeds: personalize lightly, do not paste a bare link.

## Target

A free, live Elo tracker that re-ranks every men's national team after every World Cup match, with win probabilities and a head-to-head predictor. Who loves it: fans mid-tournament asking "how much did that result actually move my team," plus the analytics, betting, and forecasting crowd who will pick apart the model.

## The 8 rooms, ranked

1. **r/dataisbeautiful** — a live gold/red ranking with history charts is legit `[OC]`. Widest reach. Post the morning after a knockout match day.
2. **X, #WorldCup2026** — "they just jumped to #X in the world" screenshot is quote-tweet fuel. Post live during a marquee match, link in a reply.
3. **Hacker News, Show HN** — the modeling crowd rewards a transparent live ratings model. Weekday 8 to 10am ET on a knockout day.
4. **r/USMNT** — USA co-hosts, engagement peaking, they live for "where do we rank now." Within a couple hours of a US full-time.
5. **r/SoccerBetting** — they will pressure-test the win probabilities. Frame it as a free model to compare, not tips. Evening before a match slate.
6. **r/algobetting** — model builders who want a documented Elo as a baseline to backtest. After a round completes.
7. **Manifold Markets, World Cup 2026 markets** — forecasters who will use "Elo says X%, market says Y%" in comments. Right after a result re-prices markets.
8. **r/socceranalytics** — smallest reach but the most on-target by purpose; they will read the methodology page.

Dropped by verification (real communities, but a raw self-promo link gets removed): r/soccer and r/worldcup (share insights inside the daily discussion threads instead, never a standalone link post), and @ESPNFC (a one-way broadcast feed, not a community).

## Don't get banned (3 rules)

1. Value first, link second. The post is a chart, an insight, or a genuine question; the link goes in a comment or reply. A bare promo link is removed.
2. Respect the 9 to 1 cadence. About nine real contributions per self-link. One room at a time, spaced days apart, never a same-day blast.
3. Say "I built this" every time. It converts self-promo suspicion into goodwill, and it is true. Never orchestrate upvotes.
