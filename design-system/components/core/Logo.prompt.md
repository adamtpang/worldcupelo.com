**Logo / Emblem** — the worldcupelo mark. The `Emblem` is a gold coin/ring holding three ascending tri-nation bars (rising Elo + the 2026 hosts CAN·MEX·USA) crowned by a gold peak-spark. `Logo` pairs it with the lower-case wordmark in Archivo Expanded, "elo" in gradient gold.

```jsx
<Logo size="md" />          {/* emblem + wordmark */}
<Logo size="lg" />
<Logo emblemOnly />         {/* mark alone — favicon, avatar */}
<Emblem size={48} />
```

Props — `Logo`: `size` (sm | md | lg), `emblemOnly`. `Emblem`: `size` (px). The wordmark is ALWAYS lower-case; never recolor the emblem. Original mark — do not substitute the official FWC26 emblem.
