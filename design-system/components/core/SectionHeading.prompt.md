**SectionHeading** — the bold tracked title + muted subtitle that opens every section. Optional right-aligned mono meta.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  title="Global Rankings"
  subtitle="All 100 men's national teams by Elo rating"
  right="Updated · Jun 2026"
/>
```

Props: `title`, `subtitle`, `size` (sm | md | lg), `right`. Titles are sentence case, not Title Case.
