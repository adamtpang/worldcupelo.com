**Chip** — the brand's small uppercase tag. Confederation chips tint a flat region color at ~16% behind its name; the gold "Host" chip flags 2026 hosts.

```jsx
<Chip variant="confederation" confederation="UEFA" />
<Chip variant="host">Host</Chip>
<Chip variant="gold">Qualified</Chip>
```

Props: `variant` (confederation | host | gold | neutral), `confederation` (UEFA…OFC — auto-fills the label). Confederation color is a TINT only, never a solid fill.
