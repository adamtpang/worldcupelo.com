**Eyebrow** — the uppercase, 0.18em-tracked micro-label that sits above headings and tags live sections. Add `live` for the pulsing emerald dot.

```jsx
<Eyebrow live>Live Elo · Every men's national team</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="gold">FIFA World Cup 2026</Eyebrow>
```

Props: `live` (pulse dot), `tone` (muted | dim | gold). Keep the text short and SHOUTING — it's a label, not a sentence.
